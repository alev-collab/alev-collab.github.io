How I work

Fluent in vibe coding and natural language architecture. Strong reading, sharp comprehension, fast learning.

Read the whole conversation first

Before changing anything, I read the full thread and the AI's own decision log. Most "AI bugs" turn out to be a rule that reads two ways.

Write rules a model can't misread

I write behaviour in plain, precise language: clear conditions, one home per rule, and the exact words automations depend on kept word for word.

Test before it touches a customer

Draft, replay against real past conversations, check edge cases that should not change, then publish and verify on live tickets the next day.

Learn the system fast

I picked up the support platform, its automation engine, AI tooling, storefront templates and reporting in weeks, by reading documentation and testing carefully.

Selected work

What I built and fixed.

AI training

Rebuilt the AI's playbook

Built and maintain 20+ conversation flows (order tracking, returns, cancellations, subscriptions, address changes), and restructured the core ones into one consistent format: guards first, then rules, then scenarios.

Fewer contradictions, faster fixes, easier hand-off to anyone new.
Safety

Guardrails that protect customers and revenue

Built checks for payment-dispute and review threats, fraud claims and repeat contacts, plus a strict "no invented facts" rule so the AI only states what it can verify.

Risky conversations reach a person fast. Routine ones don't.
Retention

Respectful save offers

Designed AI-led retention for subscription cancellations: one tailored offer, an honest confirmation when declined, and no pressure loops.

Saves where possible, with no runaround that leads to disputes.
Voice

A warmer, human tone

Rewrote the AI's voice guidelines: a short personal introduction, empathy in its own words, and room to reassure instead of clipped status updates.

AI satisfaction rose from 54% to 73%.
Automation

Reliable workflows

Audited dozens of automation rules and moved the fragile ones off customer-facing wording onto stable internal markers, so labels, routing and follow-ups keep firing.

Clean data for reporting and no silent breakages.
Help center

Self-service that answers first

We rebuilt the help center with guided contact forms that collect the right details up front, and rewrote policy articles in clear, approved language.

Storefront

Troubleshooting

First call when something's off

The team's go-to when anything in the system misbehaves: broken automations, inbox and routing settings, integrations, storefront glitches. I trace it, fix it or get it to the right person fast.

QA & reporting

A feedback loop that compounds

Ran weekly week-over-week reporting, kept a log of every AI mistake with its root cause and fix, and wrote the playbook and handover docs the team works from.
